CUSTOMER GUIDE

How to prepare a vehicle for transport

A little preparation helps the people handing off and receiving the vehicle understand what to expect.

Request a quote ↗

Confirm the details

Verify the addresses, contacts, vehicle year, make and model, and whether it starts, steers and brakes. Mention modifications, low clearance or mechanical concerns.

Get the vehicle ready

Remove loose personal items and accessories where practical. Ask the carrier for its requirements for fuel level, keys, alarms and personal belongings.

Record the condition

Take clear photos before pickup and review the carrier’s inspection report. Note existing damage and retain copies of the paperwork.

Plan for delivery

Make sure you or an authorized representative can meet the carrier. Inspect the vehicle, document any concerns on the delivery paperwork and keep the signed copy.
