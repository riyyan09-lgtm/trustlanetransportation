BUSINESS GUIDE

A guide to auction vehicle pickup

Auction pickups involve release procedures, operating hours and vehicle details that are worth checking before a transport request.

Request a quote ↗

Confirm the vehicle and payment

Record the lot number, vehicle identity, pickup location and the auction’s release requirements. Confirm that the vehicle is cleared for release before arranging collection.

Check access and timing

Ask about pickup hours, appointments, storage deadlines and any restrictions on transport equipment. Provide the pickup contact and instructions.

Plan delivery and inspection

Share the receiving address and contact, then ask the carrier how pickup and delivery inspections will be documented.

Discuss the request

Route, equipment and business service availability must be confirmed with Trustlane for each request.
