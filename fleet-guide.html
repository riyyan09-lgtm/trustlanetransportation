BUSINESS GUIDE

A guide to coordinating fleet vehicle moves

A clear vehicle list and reliable pickup and delivery contacts help organizations organize multiple moves.

Request a quote ↗

Build a vehicle list

Record each vehicle’s year, make, model, condition and identifier. Include where each unit is located and the desired destination.

Identify timing and contacts

List the person releasing each vehicle, receiving contacts, site hours and any scheduling constraints. Be clear about which dates are flexible.

Discuss equipment and access

Some vehicles or sites may need special equipment or a nearby meeting point. Ask what arrangements are available for the particular units and route.

Confirm the proposed plan

Review the quote, payment terms and responsibilities before booking. Trustlane’s availability for fleet requests must be confirmed.

Ready to discuss a move?

Share your route, vehicle and preferred date. Availability and pricing will be discussed for your request.
