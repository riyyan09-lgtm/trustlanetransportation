SHIPPING COSTS

What influences a vehicle shipping quote?

A useful quote starts with an accurate description of the route, vehicle and timing. There is no single price that fits every shipment.

Request a quote ↗

Route and access

Distance is one factor, but pickup and delivery access, route demand, and the availability of suitable equipment can also affect a proposed price.

Vehicle size and condition

A larger vehicle may occupy more carrier space. A vehicle that does not run, steer or brake can require extra handling or equipment.

Timing and transport type

Flexibility may affect the options available. Open and enclosed transport use different equipment and may have different costs where available.

Understand the complete quote

Ask what the amount includes, how long it is valid, when payment is due and what circumstances could change the price. Do not rely on an estimate until the arrangements are confirmed.
