THE PROCESS

How vehicle shipping works

From your first request to planning pickup and delivery, here are the details to discuss before you move a vehicle.

Request a quote ↗

1. Share the details

Start with the vehicle year, make and model, pickup and delivery locations, and your preferred date. Mention whether the vehicle runs and any access restrictions at either end.

2. Review the proposed arrangements

Ask about the transport option, the amount quoted, the parties involved, payment terms, and any conditions that could change the price. Availability and arrangements must be confirmed for your particular request.

3. Prepare for pickup

Remove loose items, document the vehicle’s condition and make sure keys and an authorized contact are available. Ask the carrier what documents and inspection steps are needed.

4. Inspect at delivery

Compare the vehicle with the pickup inspection and record any concerns on the delivery paperwork before signing. Keep copies of the documents for your records.
