Open and enclosed transport

Open carriers expose vehicles to the elements; enclosed equipment provides more protection from weather and road debris. Availability and cost depend on the route and equipment.

Pickup and delivery

Discuss whether the requested addresses are accessible to a large truck. Restrictions, low branches or narrow streets can make a nearby meeting point necessary.

Timing and vehicle condition

Let the team know if your dates are flexible and whether the vehicle starts, steers and brakes. Special equipment may be needed for an inoperable vehicle.
