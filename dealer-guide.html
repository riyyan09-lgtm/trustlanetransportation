BUSINESS GUIDE

A dealer’s guide to vehicle transfers

Vehicle transfers work best when the pickup party, receiving party and shipment details are aligned before a carrier is arranged.

Request a quote ↗

Confirm the vehicle and release

Record the year, make, model, VIN or stock reference, and exact pickup location. Confirm who can release the vehicle and what paperwork or identification is required.

Make pickup and delivery practical

Share gate hours, loading restrictions, keys, contact names and phone numbers. Give the receiving location enough notice to arrange an inspection and a person to accept the vehicle.

Document condition

Ask how the vehicle will be inspected at pickup and delivery. Keep copies of the inspection documents and record any concerns with the carrier at delivery.

Discuss each move

Every dealer trade or auction transfer has its own requirements. Contact Trustlane to discuss your specific needs; business service availability must be confirmed.
